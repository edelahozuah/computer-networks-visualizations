TCP diagram template

Draw the segments of a TCP connection and fill in their fields. Each row is one tick; a segment reaches the other end half a tick after it leaves. Save your exercises in the browser or export them.

How to use it

  • Click a point on the sender's time line to send a segment in that tick.
  • Fill in the fields in the sender's table, in the row of the tick it leaves.
  • Click an arrow to mark it as lost, label it, reverse it or delete it (Del).
  • Enter and the ↑ ↓ arrows move between rows; Ctrl/⌘ + S saves.
